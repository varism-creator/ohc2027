Welcome

Bridging Clinical Practice and Environmental Science

The One Health Conference 2027, organized by the Faculty of Associated Medical Sciences at Chiang Mai University, brings together researchers, clinicians, and policymakers to explore the intersection of human, animal, and environmental health. Join us in Chiang Mai for two days of scientific exchange, collaboration, and innovation.

Conference Scope

Six Pillars of Integrated Health

Climate Change & Its Impacts

Environmental shifts affecting human and animal health systems.

Antimicrobial Resistance

Tackling the growing threat of drug-resistant pathogens.

Vaccine Development

Advancing immunization strategies for emerging diseases.

Global Health Priorities

Addressing inequities in healthcare access and outcomes.

Transboundary Zoonoses

Cross-border disease transmission between animals and humans.

Innovative Research

Cutting-edge methodologies in integrated health science.

Key Dates

Registration stays open until the conference. The abstract and poster deadlines are firm.

  1. 25 September 2026 Registration opens Free registration opens for all attendees.
  2. 9 October 2026 Last day to submit an abstract Abstract submission closes at the end of the day.
  3. 12 October 2026 Acceptance notices sent Accepted presenters are notified by email.
  4. 12 – 30 October 2026 Poster files due Upload your final poster file via the emailed link.

Join Us

Register

One form covers everything, including abstracts. Registration is free and open to anyone working in clinical, laboratory, veterinary, or environmental health.

  • ✓Tell us which days you’ll join and any dietary requirements
  • ✓Choose your role: attending, presenting, or chairing
  • ✓You’ll get a confirmation email with a copy of your answers
Open the registration form

You’ll need to be signed in to a Google account.

What happens after you choose your role

  • Attending only The form ends after a few questions. Nothing to upload.
  • Presenting a talk or a poster An extra section opens where you choose your scope, enter your title and authors, and upload your abstract. There is no separate abstract form.
  • Once your abstract is accepted You’ll get an email on 12 October with your poster number and a link for uploading the poster file.

Abstracts: 250 words maximum, in English, presenting author underlined. Accepted formats are .docx and .pdf.
